## United States: honest note
- FY2027 H-1B: 211,600 registrations (down 38.5% from 343,981); USCIS has not published selection odds. Selection is now wage-weighted (final rule effective 27 Feb 2026): Level I gets 1 entry, Level IV gets 4. Only 17.7% of selections were Level I. One law firm's own results show Level I at 24.5% vs Level IV 65% (small sample, indicative only). Fee: USD 215 per registration (not re-verified).
- The Sept 2025 proclamation requires a USD 100,000 payment for H-1B petitions for beneficiaries outside the US; it was extended to Sept 2027. Courts split: it was blocked in Massachusetts (June) and N.D. Cal. (30 Sept 2026) but upheld elsewhere, with appeals pending. DHS also proposed a separate ~USD 103k fee (Aug 2026). DOL proposed higher prevailing wages (Level I rises roughly 21-33%). A proposed OPT fee (USD 70k first, 30k later) was published 8 Oct 2026.
- Verdict for a bachelor's-only candidate abroad: a direct sponsored job is very low probability. Realistic routes: a US MS then OPT then H-1B (costly, policy risk), a multinational with a US affiliate then L-1, or O-1A with strong external evidence. Indians cannot enter the green-card lottery. Safety: GPI 134 of 163.

## Kuwait and the Gulf: honest note
- **Visa**: easy. Employer applies via PAM, then degree attestation, medical, biometrics, Civil ID (6-8 weeks, secondary). I found no bachelor's or salary threshold for expats and no India-specific ban. Engineering degrees may need Kuwait Society of Engineers accreditation (current status unverified). A 2025-26 circular locks your registered degree and job title, so a mismatch can block renewal.
- **Market**: no verified size; the startup scene is small (unverified). Kuwaitization is raising employer hiring fees.
- **Pay**: KWD 600-1,200/month typical (aggregator-grade), 0% personal income tax (not officially verified), end-of-service benefit (details unverified), rent for a 1-bedroom ~KWD 285-400.
- **Life**: Kuwait was last (46 of 46) in Expat Insider 2025 overall, quality of life and ease of settling in; locals rated unfriendly, 49% negative on climate. Kafala still applies; a June 2026 reform allows early switching only for violations.
- **War risk**: since 28 Feb 2026 Iran has struck targets in Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar and the UAE (secondary). A 28 Oct 2026 report says no ceasefire; an Indian national was killed at Dammam airport. GPI was compiled before or amid the war and may understate Gulf risk.
- **Verdict**: visa-easy but weak on your criteria (not "beautiful", poor expat satisfaction, small market). If you want the Gulf, the UAE is clearly better.

## Top 5 to focus on
1. **Germany**: lowest realistic visa bar (Blue Card EUR 45,934 reduced), largest market, plus a no-offer Opportunity Card fallback. Check Osmania on anabin now.
2. **Ireland**: explicit new-grad permit rate (EUR 36,848 within 12 months of graduating), English, GPI 5, big multinational base. Time the application inside the 12-month window.
3. **Netherlands**: HSM under-30 threshold EUR 4,357/month, English workplaces, a public IND sponsor register. Apply before turning 30.
4. **UAE**: fastest visa, 0% tax, English. Accept lower pay and war-spillover risk; strong demand for AI/ML over generic software.
5. **Denmark**: software developer titles on the Positive List, high pay for juniors, English-friendly. Confirm with SIRI that a 3-year Indian B.E. counts as "IT education at bachelor level".
Backups: Austria (points), Poland (easy permit), Singapore (EP floor rising to S$6,000 in 2027).
